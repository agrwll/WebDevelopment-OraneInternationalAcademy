Our Facilities

Purpose-built spaces for hands-on training

Home / Facilities

Photographs below were taken during an academy visit as part of this Social Internship, showing the actual Badarpur campus facilities used for practical training.

Reception

A welcoming first impression

The reception area is where prospective students and visitors are greeted, and where initial enquiries and counselling conversations begin.

Hair Styling Studio

Real stations, real practice

Rows of styling chairs, mirror stations and washing points give students space to practice cutting, styling and blow-drying techniques.

Professional Makeup Lab

Studio-style makeup practice

A dedicated space with vanity stations and lighting for practicing bridal, HD and everyday makeup application.

Beauty Therapy Room

Space for hands-on therapy practice

A dedicated bench area used for beauty therapy, nail care and related practical sessions.

Skin Care Lab

Facial and skin treatment practice

Treatment beds and equipment used for skin analysis, facials and related skin care practicals.

Classroom

Where theory sessions happen

Well-lit classroom space used for theory sessions, demonstrations and technique discussions before practicals.

Training Environment

A consistent space for guided practice

Additional styling stations that support the overall practical, hands-on approach to training at the academy.

Practice Area

Connecting the campus together

The corridor links the classrooms, labs and reception, keeping the campus easy to navigate between practical sessions.
