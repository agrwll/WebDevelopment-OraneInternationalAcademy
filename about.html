Who We Are

A Badarpur campus built around practice

Orane International Beauty & Wellness Academy, Badarpur is a local training franchise offering career-oriented courses in beauty, hair, wellness and cosmetology. It works with students straight out of school, college-goers looking for a professional edge, and homemakers or working professionals ready for a career shift.

The approach is simple: fewer lectures, more hands-on hours, using dedicated labs designed to mirror real salon and studio environments.

🎯

Our Mission

To equip students with practical, job-ready skills in beauty and wellness through structured, hands-on training.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a trusted local institute for beauty & wellness education, helping beginners grow into confident, skilled professionals.

🤝

Community Impact

Supporting local students and residents of Badarpur and nearby areas in building practical, employable skills.

🌸

Women Empowerment

Encouraging women and homemakers to pursue skill-based training that opens doors to employment and self-employment.

📈

Skill Development

Focused, hands-on modules that build genuine technical ability in hair, makeup, skin care and beauty therapy.

💡

Entrepreneurship

Guidance and foundational knowledge for students interested in eventually starting their own salon or studio.
